Common questions

Does it work with no signal?
Yes. Everything — editing, printing, Show Mode — works fully offline. Changes save on your device and sync to your iCloud the next time you have a connection.
Where is my data?
On your device, and in your own private iCloud database when you are signed in to iCloud. It never touches our servers, because we do not run any. See the privacy policy.
How do I get my setlists on a new device?
Sign in with the same Apple Account and iCloud. Your library merges in on first launch — the app asks before combining two different libraries, and never silently overwrites.
How do I restore my subscription?
Tap Restore on the unlock screen, or open Settings → Account → Restore inside the app, while signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account that bought it.
How do I cancel?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Setlist Designer → Cancel. Cancelling during a free trial means nothing is ever charged.
How do I delete my data?
Delete anything inside the app, or delete the app to remove all local data. The synced copy can be removed under iCloud settings → Manage Account Storage. The privacy policy has the full steps.
What paper sizes print?
A4 and US Letter. Long sets flip to landscape automatically before text becomes unreadably small.

Report a problem

Include your device model, iOS version, and what you expected to happen. Screenshots of the setlist or Show Mode screen help a lot. We aim to reply within two working days — faster on show days.
